# ADR-0083 — The admin operations surface: ledger, appeals, strikes, escalation, webhooks

- Status: Proposed 2026-10-10; built in the same change behind migrations 0271 to 0276 (not yet applied). The webhook delivery log is behind `WEBHOOK_DELIVERY_LOG_ENABLED` ("false" in production, "true" on staging); everything else takes effect when the migrations are applied and the Worker is deployed. Owner sign-off is not yet recorded.
- Date: 2026-10-10
- Related: [ADR-0019](0019-dispute-webhooks-freeze.md) (Freeze), [ADR-0022](0022-manual-test-credit-grant.md) (manual grants need a reason naming the human), [ADR-0058](0058-byteplus-modelark-provider.md) decision 7 (warnings and takedowns), [ADR-0078](0078-admin-checks-in-code.md) (admin gates in code). Follows the 2026-10-10 research run on what an admin page for this product should see and do; the report is kept out of this repository.

## Problem

The admin page showed last-day metrics, a violations queue, a user lookup and the Cinema reviews, and could freeze or unfreeze an account. Five things an operator needs were missing, and three of them are not optional for a UK service with Cinema switched on:

1. **No ledger view.** A support ticket about a balance could only be answered by reading `ledger_entries` through the service role, and a goodwill grant was an SQL call recorded in an ADR by hand (ADR-0022).
2. **No appeal.** Ofcom's Illegal Content Codes of Practice (consolidated 9 September 2026, in force 30 September 2026) apply ICU D1, D2 and D10 to every user-to-user service regardless of size: a user must be able to contest a takedown, a warning or a suspension; a successful appeal must reverse the action; a pattern of wrong decisions must change the guidance. Cinema publishing and viewing are on in production, so the user-to-user test is met.
3. **One strike tier.** Three takedowns froze the account; nothing happened in between, and a takedown later found wrong still counted.
4. **No CSAM path.** The Thorn / All Tech Is Human *Safety by Design for Generative AI* standard (signed by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, Microsoft, Stability) asks a deployer to detect, to route to a moderation flow, to document the report-preserve-destroy procedure, to give users a real-time report path, and to enforce at user and model level. A takedown deleted the evidence the report needs.
5. **No view of refused webhooks.** A delivery refused at the door left a console line.

## Decision

Everything below is admin-only in the database (`users.is_admin`, raised as 42501 `not_admin`), behind the four gates of ADR-0078 in the route (`lib/adminGate.js`), and written to the append-only `account_actions` log with the admin's email as actor and a reason. Nothing is edited or deleted; corrections are new rows.

1. **Ledger drilldown and reasoned grant (0271).** `admin_user_ledger` returns one account's balance buckets, every ledger row with its reason and the action log. `admin_credit_grant` appends a Pack grant through `ledger_grant` with reason `grant:admin <email>` and the form's Idempotency-Key, and records `credit_grant` on `account_actions` with the operator's note, the ledger row and the amount. There is no negative path: Free and Subscription Credits have their own lifecycle and a Pack clawback is a separate decision. The note is for administrators; the user's statement files the row as an adjustment.
2. **Appeals (0272, 0273).** `content_appeals` holds one appeal per `account_actions` row for a warning, a takedown or a `content:` Freeze: the user's statement, and one decision with a reason. A user submits from `/app/account/appeals`; an admin decides at `/app/admin/appeals`. The decision is written to `account_actions` as `appeal_upheld` or `appeal_rejected`. Upholding a takedown takes it out of the strike count, recomputes the hold from the strikes that stand, and lifts a `content:` Freeze through `unfreeze_account` when fewer than three remain. Files already deleted are not restored. The user is emailed the outcome without the reason. The CSAM tier is not appealable.
3. **Strike ladder and generation hold (0272, 0274).** A takedown that stands is a strike. Strike 1 sets `users.hold_until` three days out, strike 2 seven days, strike 3 Freezes as before. A hold is never shortened by a later strike. The two admission functions (`private.ledger_debit_for_admission`, `private.submit_free_job_for_admission`) refuse `ACCOUNT_ON_HOLD` while the hold is in the future, so every paid and free generation path obeys it; buying credits, the Library and the account page do not. The model is Google Ads' strike ladder; the windowing is ours (no decay, standing strikes only).
4. **CSAM escalation and evidence hold (0275).** `record_csam_escalation` deletes the job's `assets` rows so nothing is served, moves the R2 keys to `evidence_holds` instead of the reap queue, removes them from the queue if already there, and Freezes the account with reason `content:csam_escalation`. No email is sent for this tier. `release_evidence_hold` writes `evidence_released` and queues the keys when the procedure allows. The procedure is `docs/agents/csam-escalation.md`: IWF or NCMEC within 24 hours, preserve 90 days or as long as law enforcement asks, record identifiers only.
5. **User reports (0275).** `content_reports`: a signed-in user reports a generation of their own (Report on the Library card), a Cinema title, or anything else, with a category; one open report per subject, at most twenty open per person. `/app/admin/reports` lists them CSAM first and closes each with a note.
6. **Webhook delivery log (0276).** `withDeliveryLog` wraps every inbound webhook route. After the handler answered, one row goes to `webhook_deliveries`: source, outcome class, HTTP status, typed error code. No payload, no headers. The answer the provider sees is never changed by the log, and a logging failure is swallowed. `/app/admin/webhooks` shows per-source counts by outcome, the refusals with the fix for each class, and `webhook_events` rows accepted but never processed. Rows older than thirty days are pruned opportunistically.

## Consequences

- Six migrations, all idempotent, none touching the money invariants: `reconcile_balances`, `reconcile_free_credits` and `reconcile_subscription_credits` return zero rows after a grant, a hold, an appeal and an escalation in the local smoke tests.
- `account_actions.action` gains `credit_grant`, `appeal_upheld`, `appeal_rejected`, `csam_escalation`, `evidence_released`; the table gains `ledger_entry_id`, `credits_delta`, `hold_until`. `users` gains `hold_until`.
- `record_content_violation` keeps its 0253 signature and answer shape, plus `hold_until`; `admin_lookup_user` and `list_content_violations` keep theirs with more fields. The violations page shows standing strikes, reversed ones, open appeals and the hold.
- `ACCOUNT_ON_HOLD` is a new typed refusal (403) from the generation, chat and fal dispatch paths, with its own copy in the create page and the chat.
- Every notice email now points at the appeal page; a takedown says until when generating is paused.
- Not built, on purpose: automatic scanning of prompts, uploads or outputs (a provider or hash-list decision with its own cost and agreement); a Pack clawback; moderator SLA dashboards and the other Ofcom measures that apply only above 7 million UK monthly users or to a multi-risk service; a bespoke creator payout view (Cinema payouts do not exist yet, ADR-0057).
- Open for the owner: whether the illegal-harms risk assessment makes this service multi-risk (which pulls ICU C3 to C8 in) or at medium or high risk of any single harm (which pulls D4 and D6, indicative complaint timeframes, in); whether a second administrator and two-person approval above a grant threshold are wanted before any grant is made in production.
