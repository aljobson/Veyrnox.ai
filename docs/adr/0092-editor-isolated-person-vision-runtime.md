# ADR-0092 — Isolated local person segmentation and motion processing

Status: implemented and qualified with bounded native local journeys. Request-time `EDITOR_LOCAL_INFERENCE_ENABLED` separately gates the person tools; production stays off. Motion tools do not require a model.

## Decision

Create original bounded local picture operations that produce a distinct MP4 source while preserving the selected original. The editor imports the result through its existing media probe; the project JSON schema does not change. Cancellation discards partial output. The editor must cancel when the account, selected source or editing context changes. Completed results are ordinary local files, with the existing relink and project-media-upload rules.

Motion analysis estimates actual two-axis translation using textured blocks and zero-mean normalized correlation. Confidence filtering, robust global motion and shot-local trajectory smoothing drive stabilization. Uniform temporal luminance correction reduces exposure flicker. Compression can erase fine texture in one exposure and cause a false cut: only the flicker tool may veto a suspected cut when coarse exposure-normalized structure correlates at least 0.6 and mean exposure changes by at least 16%. Other motion tools retain their cut decision. Similar-looking editorial cuts and intentional flashes still require manual review. Bidirectional block optical flow warps pictures toward intermediate positions, checking forward/backward consistency; uncertain areas and cuts retain a nearest source picture. Selected-region tracking follows texture and retains the last reliable framing when tracking is lost. Manual camera movement is an original animated crop, with no generated scene content.

Picture processing uses the existing pinned Mediabunny 1.60.0 and browser WebCodecs. The initial limits are 100 MiB input, 30 seconds for ordinary motion, 10 seconds for optical flow and person tools, 1,800 output pictures, 720-pixel output height and 1,280-pixel output width. Source video is limited to 4,096 pixels. Existing AAC packets, priming and timing are remuxed rather than re-encoded; other source audio codecs are explicitly refused. Silent video is supported. Outputs keep source duration and sound pitch. Stabilization corrects translation only; rotation, rolling shutter, parallax and large shake can require substantial crops or limited corrections. Block flow is bounded to a 160-pixel analysis edge and cannot promise high-quality slow motion for fast movement or occlusion.

Person tools use the existing pinned MediaPipe Tasks Vision 1.0.1 with the explicitly licensed Selfie Multiclass 256×256 model, version 1. Its six classes drive transparent background removal for silent videos, solid-background replacement, face/body skin smoothing with optional skin-only light adjustment, an original silhouette glow and automatic framing around the largest separate person mask. A smooth confidence matte maps foreground probability 0.2–0.8 to alpha 0–1, removing the model's low-confidence background haze while keeping soft edges. Reframing selects the largest connected person first; confident face pixels inside that same person anchor horizontal framing when at least eight pixels and 0.3% of the mask qualify at confidence 0.6. Weak or missing face evidence retains the whole-person centre. These are actual local semantic measurements. No face/body reshaping, arbitrary object recognition, identity matching, generative relighting or licensed CapCut asset is implied. Overlapping people can share a mask. Hair, rapid movement, noise, low light, backlighting and occlusion reduce mask quality.
