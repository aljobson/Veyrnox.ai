# Crun Suno as the Music Create supplier, fal as standby (ADR-0376)

Status: Built, dark. Nothing in this ADR activates Crun. Activation is an owner-run runbook (`docs/runbooks/crun-suno-music-activation.md`) gated on Crun's written answer and a paid probe.

## Context

On 2026-11-22 every production `ace-step` song pair failed `provider_submit_failed`: fal answered a 4xx about 0.3 s after the durable dispatch sent each job. The root cause was not captured (the fal error text is only logged by the Worker). The owner chose Crun's Suno API (`suno/music-generate`) as route 1 for song creation, fal as standby, a price of at least 50% gross margin, and a Create form that mirrors Crun's own Suno form.

Facts the design rests on (Crun docs, read in full 2026-11-22): `CreateTask` has no idempotency key and tasks can only be looked up by `task_id`; 20 creates per 10 s and 15 status reads per second per account; HTTP 200 and body code 200 are both required for success; 401/402/403 are account-level, 455/505 route-level, 422/451 input, 429 rate, 501 a failed task (charges 0); callbacks are unsigned with undocumented retries; output is the `suno_data[].suno_audio_url` file (examples are MP3, format not guaranteed); generated media is deleted after about 14 days; submitted text is deleted after 1 hour but logs keep it 2 months; support is UTC 21:00-17:00 with no SLA. Crun's Terms 6.2 limit credits to images and videos and say nothing about music or resale.

## Decision

1. **Scope.** Only the song-create pair for the offers `ace-step` and `ace-step-1.5`. Extend, replace, add-section, loop, stems and cover keep their providers. Not in v1: a shared Crun task across both candidates, Suno extend/cover/split/persona, persisted chat-thread song messages, automatic failover to fal.
2. **Durable dispatch.** `crun_dispatch` mirrors `fal_dispatch`: the immutable request payload is frozen in the debit transaction (migration 0591), a fenced consumer claims and submits once, and a paid call is never retried. A stale STARTED row becomes UNKNOWN, never READY. The request route only calls `music_submit_pair` and publishes a wake-up; there is no request-lifetime paid submission.
   The Crun wake-up is intentionally a no-op in this version: the existing queue worker has no Crun credential. A pair therefore waits up to roughly 60 seconds for the per-minute backstop, plus provider time. This accepted latency can be removed later by wiring that queue to the same fenced claim path.
3. **Failure classes.** 422/451 refund the candidate; 401/402/403 pause the account circuit (the existing `provider_spend_provider_policy` Crun row) and return the job to READY; 455/505 trip every `crun:suno/music-generate` route; a disabled Suno model trips an (endpoint, model) breaker; 429 defers with progressive backoff then refunds as `supplier_busy`; 5xx, timeout and transport are UNKNOWN. A READY row held by a circuit refunds after 20 minutes (nothing was ever sent). TaskInfo 501 refunds immediately. An accepted task is never refunded because time passed or status could not be read: unreadable holds and alerts, and a task still pending after 45 minutes goes to an operator.
4. **UNKNOWN has no deterministic recovery** (no idempotency key, lookup by task id only). Policy: alert on every UNKNOWN; after 10 minutes the operator resolves it with `reconcile_crun_dispatch_unknown`, normally as REJECTED (fenced refund), accepting at most one wasted $0.0536 task per job, or as ACCEPTED when Crun's logs page shows the task id.
5. **Shared boundary.** One pre-submit boundary (`lib/crunSubmitBoundary.js`) is used by every Crun caller, image, video, swap and music: a database sliding-window limiter (at most 20 creates in any rolling 10 s), the account circuit and the route breakers. TaskInfo polling is paced account-wide (at most 15 per second) across the sweep and check-on-read. Every Crun model returns only typed codes plus HTTP/body numbers, never vendor message text; swap, Avatar and Seedance Reference keep their reviewed reason slug in the legacy `crun <http>/<code>: <reason>` string.
6. **Per-offer contracts.** Song-create prompt specs, drafts and quotes carry an immutable `provider_contract` (`fal-ace-v1` or `crun-suno-v1`) with limits keyed to it, so flipping a route never changes an old row's meaning. Crun limits: prompt 500, title 80, lyrics 5000 (required for custom vocal), styles 1000, exclude styles 1000 (only 200 applied), duration 1-135 s, variety 0-4, style weight 0-1, weirdness 0-1, vocal gender; BPM, key and time signature are not offered. fal limits are unchanged. Drafts are unique per (user, contract); legacy signatures stay as active-contract wrappers until a later migration (expand/contract). Entitlements and quote validation read the catalog fresh, not through the 5-minute cache. The wire carries the contract and a draft identity; stale ones return `CONTRACT_STALE`/`QUOTE_STALE`.
7. **Price.** At least 50% gross margin means price at least twice cost. Suno costs $0.0536 per request; at the $0.033 reference credit rate that is 4 credits per candidate (8 per pair, 59.4% margin). 3 credits would be 45.9%. The repository's `MARGIN_FLOOR = 0.5` is a markup and would pass 3, so the runbook asserts `credits x 0.033 >= 2 x cost`. Price is flat in duration. The cost is Crun's published page, not an invoice: re-verify on the first bill.
8. **Assets and actions.** Songs are stored as returned. The audio host allowlist ships EMPTY (settlement fails closed with a typed alert) until the paid probe captures the real host; the real MIME is detected from Content-Type and magic bytes and recorded on the version. Edit actions follow the current version's MIME and size, not the original provider: local PCM render/crop/remix/mashup/sample need canonical WAV up to 26,214,400 bytes and return `edit_unavailable_for_format` (409) otherwise; stems accept MP3; other provider-backed actions stay enabled only where their RPCs and providers accept the file.
9. **Routes and activation.** Crun and fal are `model_routes` rows beneath the existing catalog offers; Crun ships standby/unverified. Migrations 0591-0593 are applied by the normal workflow and change no behaviour. Activation and rollback are reviewed transactional runbook SQL run by the owner, never a committed migration.
10. **UI.** The Create studio and the chat composer Audio tab mirror Crun's Suno form from the server's entitlements projection only (limits, counters, price). Results render in the existing candidate cards; chat-thread song messages need their own table and ADR.

## Consequences

- Songs cost 4 credits per candidate on Crun against 2-3 today, and a pair costs about $0.107 of supplier spend against about $0.024 on fal.
- Crun songs are MP3: local editing is unavailable for them until a decoder or a dual asset ships.
- UNKNOWN submits need an operator and can cost one wasted Crun task each.
- Crun's Terms do not cover music or resale and its logs hold prompts and lyrics for two months: the owner must obtain a written answer and update the privacy policy and sub-processor list before activation.
- The fal root cause is still unconfirmed and still affects fal-backed extend, replace and loop paths.

## Verification

Unit and acceptance tests cover the adapter classifiers, the dispatch state machine, limiter and circuit, the sweep hold rules, contract wire identity, byte caps, the format-aware actions and the UI behaviour. Acceptance tests run against a fully replayed database (CI order) and the account deletion, job deletion, cancel and recovery-health scripts. A paid staging probe (owner-approved) is still required: capture the audio host, confirm the price with `estimate-credits`, store and play one song, and force one refusal.
