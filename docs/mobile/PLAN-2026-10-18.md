## 4. Risks worth watching

1. **Review rejection on content:** Apple 1.2 (UGC) and the Google AI-content policy. Cinema comments and anything showing another person's content wait for S10.
2. **Billing cap:** production vars are at the Cloudflare limit; every new mobile flag needs a slot first.
3. **Carrier IPs and rate limits:** mobile networks share addresses; the app does not parse `Retry-After` yet.
4. **Unverified on device:** Turnstile, RevenueCat sandbox, HLS under signed tokens. Each has a fallback (web view, test account, iframe) but none is proven.
5. **One developer identity:** the wallet and this app share the Apple team and Play account (owner decision). The hard wall in `CLAUDE.md` still applies to every screen and store listing.
