## Critical correction: BytePlus packs

The current official resource-pack rules apply deduction multipliers. Seedance 2.0 Fast without video input: $3.30 per million pack tokens × 1.697 ≈ $5.60 per million consumed tokens. Seedance 2.5 without video input, 480p/720p: $6.40 × 1.671875 = $10.70. The old survey assumed 1:1 deduction and its blanket 40% saving must not be used for purchases or route activation. Promotional PAYG prices are separate. Packs expire after 90 days and are non-refundable.

## Bulk buying and competing suppliers

- KIE advertises a 10% credit bonus on a $1,250 top-up. This is a 9.09% unit-cost reduction if all credits are used: rate / 1.10. It is not a 10% price cut. This is published prepaid pricing, not a negotiated wholesale contract.
- Higgsfield publishes discounted API rates, but configuration and maximum-discount eligibility must be established. Its consumer subscriptions are separate. Do not treat its temporary cashback as a permanent zero-cost supply contract.
- MuAPI, Atlas Cloud, Runware, WaveSpeed and fal remain quote candidates. The prior survey has configuration ambiguities and conflicting rates; no global cheapest claim follows from it.
- A price below the original vendor list does not, by itself, prove unofficial sourcing. Verify contractual supply and platform rights separately.
- Request comparable quotes at $500/$1,250/$5,000 monthly usage, exact model version, resolution, duration, audio and input features, with expiry, failed-job refunds, concurrency and platform use rights.
