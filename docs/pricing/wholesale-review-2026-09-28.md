## Critical correction: BytePlus packs

The current official resource-pack rules apply deduction multipliers. Seedance 2.0 Fast without video input: $3.30 per million pack tokens × 1.697 ≈ $5.60 per million consumed tokens. Seedance 2.5 without video input, 480p/720p: $6.40 × 1.671875 = $10.70. The old survey assumed 1:1 deduction and its blanket 40% saving must not be used for purchases or route activation. Promotional PAYG prices are separate. Packs expire after 90 days and are non-refundable.
