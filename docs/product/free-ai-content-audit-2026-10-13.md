# Free AI content audit — 13 October 2026

## Result

Four short-form assets passed the legal, technical, and visual gate and are published in Social Cinema:

1. **Late Afternoon Swans (CogVideoX)** — approved.
2. **Painting With The Breeze (CogVideoX)** — approved.
3. **Grey Haired Man in a Cosy Cafe (Veo 3)** — approved and published from a silent derivative.
4. **Visual Kei Man Enjoys Lemonade (Veo 3)** — approved and published from a silent derivative.

Both are 6.125-second, silent VP9/WebM files at 720×480. The Commons contributor, VulcanSphere, expressly applied CC0 1.0 in jurisdictions that recognise copyright in computer-generated works. The CogVideoX licence states that intellectual-property rights in generated content belong to the user to the extent local law permits. Its separate registration requirement governs commercial use of the 5B model; Social Cinema will redistribute the already-generated, independently licensed output and will not operate or distribute the model.

The clips were reviewed frame-by-frame using contact sheets. Neither contains a watermark, recognisable real person, brand, copyrighted character, speech, or music. Their low resolution and six-second duration make them suitable as an **AI Visual Studies** pilot collection, not as feature films.

The two Veo 3 candidates are 7.999-second, 1280×720 VP9/WebM files. VulcanSphere also applied CC0 1.0 to these files. Social Cinema removed their generated audio because the source pages did not separately document the soundtrack rights. The video frames and visible Veo provenance watermark were otherwise preserved. The catalogue synopsis records both the original-source hash and the silent stream-master hash.

## Blocked after inspection

### Cyberpunk Rainy Street

The file has a visible `MINIMAX | Hailuo AI` watermark. Hailuo's commercial rights depend on the generation plan, so the uploader's CC0 declaration does not establish that the uploader received commercial output rights from the tool. Obtain dated creator confirmation of the plan and the applicable terms.

### AI generated video clip — New creative video

The file has a visible D-ID watermark. D-ID's current guidance restricts Trial, Lite, and Build outputs to personal use; commercial rights begin with higher paid plans. The mark is consistent with a restricted tier. Obtain dated creator confirmation before use.

### Micronauts observing a pollen snowman / collecting pollen

The creators applied CC BY-SA 4.0 to their own work and identify the real microscopy source as their own, but the pages do not identify the AI tool or document the included stereo soundtrack. The visuals are suitable; the generation and audio components need written provenance.

## Publication package

All four approved titles were created as `SHORT`. Use factual titles, select every relevant AI disclosure, include the courtesy attribution and source URL in the synopsis, and preserve the downloaded source masters, transformation records, and SHA-256 values above.

The current product does not have dedicated public licence fields. Until those are implemented, the synopsis must carry the source, creator, licence, and modifications. A second administrator should compare each submission with the manifest before publication.
