Checked 13 October 2026. “Free to watch” does not by itself grant Veyrnox permission to copy or re-host a video. The catalog may use an official provider player when embedding is enabled; storing a copy requires an explicit redistribution licence or creator agreement.

The active catalog is curated for substantial seasons: every series has at least six episodes, and every listed episode is at least eight minutes long. Smaller series and shorter clip-style episodes are excluded.

## Provider rules for the active YouTube path

- Use the official YouTube iframe player and show YouTube as the source.
- Do not download, cache, store, modify or provide offline playback of YouTube audiovisual content.
- Do not cover player controls or ads, gate the player behind payment, or reward views.
- Send an HTTP referrer and keep the player at least 200 × 200 pixels.
- Link Veyrnox Terms to the YouTube Terms and describe embedded-player data use in the Privacy Policy.
