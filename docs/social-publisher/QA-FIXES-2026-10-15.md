# Publish QA repairs — 15 October 2026

The production acceptance pass found three functional defects and several team-role UX issues.

- SmartLink saves deleted/recreated destination rows, cascading away their click counts. Migration 0324 preserves IDs during edits/reordering, transfers removed links' historical clicks into the page total, validates submitted IDs against the same page, and serializes clicks with saves. Older clients retain exact matching destinations. The editor retains IDs and reloads its persisted counters after saving. Previously erased history cannot be reconstructed.
- RSS worker claims embedded a modifying CTE inside a JSON subquery, which PostgreSQL rejects. Migration 0325 executes the claim at the top level, retaining bounded batches, due-time advancement and SKIP LOCKED semantics.
- Analytics used a teammate's personal billing plan. It now uses the selected brand's already-authorized accounts response. Calendar also removes its redundant personal-plan lookup. Billing endpoints remain personal and owner-only.
- Revoked brand selections recover GET reads once under the caller's current/default brand. Writes are never retried under a different brand. Invitations recognize stored identity while a token awaits refresh, and the acceptance link opens Publish rather than a management-only page. Viewer history no longer offers an inert reuse action.
- Contact imports say “imported” rather than “added”, since repeat CSV imports update existing records.
