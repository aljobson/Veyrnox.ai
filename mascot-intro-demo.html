Vey mascot intro

The mascot leaps in from the left, runs along VEYRNOX one capital at a time, jumps over the full stop onto the a, then hops onto the dotless ı and stays there as the dot of the i. This is a browser version of the app's MascotWordmark; arms and legs run on the same stride cycle as the app (540 ms per step) and stop once Vey has landed.

  • Leap inFades in off the left edge, 520 ms.
  • RunOne quick stride per letter. Each capital sinks under the weight.
  • Final jumpOver the full stop, onto the a, then onto the ı, 420 ms.
  • DotShrinks to about half size and squashes slightly as it lands.
