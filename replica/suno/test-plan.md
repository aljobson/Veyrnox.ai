## Exit criteria

- Every recon flow has a numbered happy, edge, and negative case below.
- Every implemented proof-slice path passes with no unexpected console error, failed request, or HTTP 5xx.
- Implemented screens pass Axe, keyboard, and 390 px overflow checks.
- Credit debits/refunds and pair idempotency are asserted from durable state.
- No browser request reaches Suno, another music provider, or a disabled Music backend.
- No open S0/S1 defect remains. Implemented-slice S2 defects are fixed before handoff.

## Cross-flow edge matrix

- Empty, whitespace, minimum and exact maximum inputs: executed for F02/F03; planned where applicable elsewhere.
- Unicode, emoji, accents and inert markup: executed at the F02 boundary and across all F03 draft fields.
- Rapid repeated action and idempotent retry: executed for F02 and F12, including lost acknowledgements for workspace creation and atomic movement.
- Refresh, Back and two tabs: refresh/two-tab executed for F02/F03/F12, including three-way draft merge; Back is planned for each editor and confirmation path.
- Slow/offline/timeout: transient 502 polling recovery, a lost confirmation acknowledgement, draft-load recovery and failed-autosave retry are executed; provider latency still requires staging.
- Expired session and second user: expired-session no-request behavior and forged-auth rejection are executed; F12 rejects hidden/disappeared records in the browser contract, while the database acceptance suite specifies mixed-owner rollback and requires a disposable migrated database to run.
- Timezone/DST: required for invitations, quotas, expiry and publications once those flows exist.
- Mobile, keyboard and screen reader: executed on the proof slice; Studio remains explicitly desktop/tablet gated.
